The tower has been watching over campus for years. Now it has a website.

Tower 212 is a fan site with a simple job: put the best of Bulls Country in one place, and keep it fresh without anyone babysitting it.

## What you will find here

- **Scores and schedule.** Results, the next kickoff, and where to watch, updated on their own.
- **Podcasts and news.** New episodes and headlines from the shows and outlets that cover USF, pulled in every hour. Every link goes to the original.
- **Free downloads.** Wallpapers for every screen, memes, sticker packs, and icon packs. No account, no paywall.
- **The blog.** Tower lore, stadium shade models, rankings, and whatever else the view from 212 feet inspires.

## Talk about it

There is no comment section here on purpose. The conversation already happens on X and Discord, so every post links straight there. Tag [@WaterTowerUSF](https://x.com/WaterTowerUSF) and say hello.

Go Bulls.
